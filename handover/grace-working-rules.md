# How Grace ran PETL with Claude (working rules)

**Writing and review**
- Scripts follow Grace's PETL rules: source-pulls/_exports/grace-claude-project-2026-10-27/petl-claude-code/docs/04-writing-rules.md. UGC runs 90 to 120 seconds; in-store us-vs-them demos run short (about 55 to 60 seconds) and get straight to the comparison.
- Every hook gets its own 5 to 8 word text overlay that adds to the spoken hook, not repeats it. "Best gift for [person] 2026" is a format that works.
- For review rounds, paste the full scripts in chat. Nothing goes to Notion until approved.
- Every draft runs the grounding check (facts and claims) and the voice check before review.
- No em dashes. American English.

**Iterations**
- Iterate the client's top ads in the account over the last 30 days, not only NSY's.
- Keep the proven script and visuals. Change only the one named variable (hook, voice, banner, pacing).
- Old prices are the only other edit: $49 or $50 becomes $64.95, "under $50" becomes "under $70."
- Don't pitch new angles or rewrite lines on an iteration. If a premise breaks, flag it in one line.
- Live ads stay as they are when rules or prices change. Brand claim rules apply to new ads.
- On editor-only briefs (AI VO, hook stacks, banners), leave hook visuals to the editor ("Editor's choice").

**Casting**
- 5 to 7 creators a batch, 2 or 3 concepts each, no repeats from the batch before.
- Sourcing briefs give general criteria (availability, setting, diversity, couples), not one specialist per concept. Actors play roles.

**Notion**
- Master Creative Database briefs: fill every field (strategist, edit reviewer, Urgent, Net New or Iteration, Creator Needed, Creator(s) linked from the Creator Database, Description, Hypothesis, ICP, Brief Link, a "Brief Done" Time Log row). Concept numbers are the next free number in brief order.
- Ideas Library entries: 📺 for video, 🖼️ for static, title prefix "R<round> · ", copy existing entries' properties.
- Notion's database queries have an hourly limit on this workspace. When it's hit, use search and page fetches instead.

**Offer and claims (as of 2026-11-03)**
- Black Friday offer = the standing ladder, up to 30% off (2+ 20%, 3+ 25%, 5+ 30%). Frame it as the biggest discount of the year. Rakesh signs off occasion wording.
- Claim gates are in brand-lens.md (set Watch and AirPods "on top," never "at the same time"; "try it risk-free for 30 days," never "full refund"; "4.8 stars from over 1,400 reviews"; original = top-up, PETL 2 Plus = nearly a full charge on an iPhone 16 Pro).
