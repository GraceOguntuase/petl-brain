## Voice and language

**Two registers, and they are not the same.** The brand's site speaks in clipped Apple-style fragments ("Snap. Charge. Go.", "No apps. No setup. It just works."). The ads that win speak like a real person talking. Scripts follow the ad register, not the site register. *(verified site copy · BIA, 2026-10-27; stated rule · docs/04 §B)*

- **Spoken, not written.** Short declarative sentences, lines that start with "and" or "but," honest concessions stated flat ("It works, but then you're tied to the wall."). PETL65 is the register benchmark for older audiences. *(stated · Grace, docs/04 §B)*
- **American English only.** Mom not mum, outlet not plug socket, guy not bloke, aluminum, color, favorite. Some older approved briefs slipped into British spelling; fix it when reusing them. *(stated · Grace, docs/04 §B)*
- **No em dashes or spaced hyphens in scripts.** Grace: they make it "look AI-generated." *(stated · docs/04 §B)*
- **Banned AI tells:** time-stamped symptoms ("dead by 2pm," "30% by lunch"), three-fragment runs ("Always charged. Always ready. Always there."), "here's the thing," "let that sink in," "for example," "if I'm honest" asides, self-aware literary lines. *(stated · Grace, docs/04 §B)*
- **Banned brand-speak and greeting-card lines:** "peace of mind," "because you can't be there," "power, perfected," "Designed to belong," "Premium In. Premium Out.," "Apple gave up. We didn't." *(stated · docs/04 §B, §G)*
- **Grace keeps a general prose-style checklist,** "BANNED - THE DEFINITIVE GUIDE" (Ben's list, run through the `de-ai-ify` skill), as an extra AI-cliché pass. It is a general style source, not a PETL rule set, and its fiction and erotica sections don't apply. File: `source-pulls/_exports/grace-claude-project-2026-10-27/BANNED - THE DEFINITIVE GUIDE.txt`. *(stated · docs/04 §B)*

**Naming the product**
- **Always "PETL 2 Plus" in full.** Never "PETL Plus" or "Plus" on its own in copy, even though the brand's own site selector and nav say "PETL PLUS." *(stated · PETL124 brief and docs/01; site break verified · BIA, 2026-10-27)*
- **Never call PETL a "thing" or a "circle."** A circle could be anything. Say "this little circular power bank," "this tiny charger," "magnetic power bank," "this viral little power bank." *(stated · PETL104 brief; docs/04 §B)*
- Pronounced "PETAL." *(stated · onboarding form, 2026-01-12)*
- **"MagSafe-compatible,"** never "MagSafe battery pack" or "MagSafe®" as a product noun in creative. *(stated · PETL68 editor notes; digest §4)*
- **Say "iPhone," not "phone,"** when compatibility matters, because it only works with iPhone. Say **"snap it on,"** not "stick it on." "Apple Watch," never "iWatch." *(stated · docs/04 §B)*
- **Size language that's true:** "almost the size of a golf ball," "smaller than your palm," "barely bigger than a powder compact," "fits in your coin pocket." Never "smaller than a golf ball" or "smaller than an Apple Watch face." *(stated · docs/01, docs/04; spec 70mm verified · WPA)*
- **Battery language:** "about another ten hours" of talk time for the original, not "charges up to 10 hours." *(stated · docs/04 §B, docs/01)*

## Claims and compliance constraints

Hard rules. These outrank craft. The full working table is WPA "Claims safe to use in scripts"; this section holds the gates.

**Apple affiliation (the client's only written restriction)**
- Never "Apple-approved," "Apple-certified," "Made for Apple," "Official Apple accessory," "Apple-made," "Apple-endorsed." "Compatible with iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods" only in a factual compatibility context. *(stated · onboarding form, 2026-01-12; NSY extension, PETL68 editor notes)* Apple branding is fine when it's just there in a real setting, like filming inside an Apple Store (PETL47 already ran with the Apple logo visible). What's banned is implying Apple endorses PETL, not showing Apple. *(stated · Grace, 2026-10-27)*
- **Never "Made for iPhone" in any form,** even though the live homepage says "Made for iPhone Pro & Pro Max." It is Apple's licensing badge and sits next to the banned list. Flag to Rakesh, don't copy. *(verified homepage · WPA, 2026-10-27; avoidance inferred)*
- Carry "PETL is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc." on Apple-led statics. *(stated · docs/01; footer verified · BIA)*
- Competitor logos blurred, or brand names in text only. Default to blur (PETL47 ran with the Apple logo visible; that's the exception, not the rule). *(stated · PETL51 rule; docs/08)*

**Battery and charging**
- **The original 3,000mAh PETL is a top-up, never a "full charge."** The brand's own table shows about 55% of an iPhone 16 Pro over MagSafe. Say "a top-up that gets you through the day." Never "full charge," "charges to 100%." *(verified · brand performance guide, WPA, 2026-10-27)*
- **PETL 2 Plus: "nearly a full charge on an iPhone 16 Pro,"** not "a full extra charge." The table shows 92%. *(verified · WPA; the PRWeb "full extra charge" line is undercut by the brand's own figure)*
- **"Up to 15 extra hours" for PETL 2 Plus: verify before use.** It's in the PETL124 brief and docs/01, but the live Plus PDP read on 2026-10-27 shows "10+ Extra hours." **"+17 hours" is wrong.** *(stated vs verified conflict · WPA)*
- **Apple Watch and AirPods: "set your Apple Watch or AirPods on top."** Never "all at the same time," "every Apple Watch," or "via cable." The live site describes Watch charging three different ways, and "at the same time" isn't supported. "Charges your iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods" is always safe. *(verified contradiction · WPA, 2026-10-27; open with Rakesh)*
- **Qi2:** "meets the Qi2 standard" only, never "Qi2 certified" or "certified Qi." The site itself never claims Qi2. *(stated · docs/01; verified absence · WPA)*
- No "stays cool" or "never gets hot" (the brand blog admits warmth); say "built-in thermal sensors." No "improves your battery health." No charging-time claims ("full in under an hour"). *(stated · docs/01; WPA)*

**Travel**
- **No in-flight use.** Say "airplane-safe, fine in your carry-on." Never "use it in-flight." Some airlines and ICAO now limit in-flight power bank use. *(stated · WPA, 2026-10-27)*
- No "airlines are going paperless" (only Ryanair fully); "your boarding pass is on your phone" is true and stronger. Never name an airline. *(stated · docs/01, docs/08)*

**Offer, price, returns**
- **"Try it risk-free for 30 days" is approved.** **Never "full refund," "30-day full refund," "no questions asked," or "free returns."** The refund policy deducts $5, keeps original shipping, and needs unused condition and full bundles back. Batch 7 briefs PETL111, 118, 119, 122, 124, 126 and 127 close on "30-day full refund" and need fixing before they launch. *(30-day claim stated · Grace, 2026-09-25; policy verified · WPA, 2026-10-27)*
- **Live prices:** PETL $64.95, PETL 2 Plus $89.95. Whole-order ladder 2+ = 20%, 3+ = 25% ("MOST POPULAR"), 5+ = 30% ("BEST VALUE"). "Up to 30% off" is accurate. *(verified · live PDP, WPA, 2026-10-27; matches PETL112 brief, 2026-10-06)*
- **Honest anchors:** "under $70" for one original; "get 3 and they're under $50 each" ($48.72). "Your third one costs $32" holds only for the original, two to three units. *(verified arithmetic · WPA)*
- **Free shipping = $129+ cart value.** Original needs 3+, PETL 2 Plus qualifies at 2+. Never "free shipping on 2+" as a blanket claim. Ship-speed line: "ships same day if you order before noon Eastern." Not "free express" or "free FedEx 2Day." *(verified · live PDP, WPA, 2026-10-27)*
- **PETL15 is dead.** "THE PETL15 DISCOUNT CODE NO LONGER EXISTS." Also dead: "15% off," "$49," single-unit "under $50," "half the price of Apple's," "Free 2-Day Shipping." *(stated · Grace, PETL68 editor notes, 2026-09-08)* A live ad still ran a PETL15 "today only" code as of 2026-10-12; escalate to Charley or Rakesh. *(verified · Meta Ad Library via external research)*
- WELCOME15 is live in the site pop-up but has never been approved for creative. Don't use it or stack it with bundles. *(verified pop-up · WPA)*
- Don't show the full price next to "20% off." Don't put a contested stat alone on screen. *(stated · docs/04 §D)*
- "Tariffs" may be named as the reason prices rise. No political figures. **No date on a price rise until Rakesh confirms in writing.** *(stated · docs/01)*
- **Urgency must be real.** No invented deadlines or countdowns. Occasion wording ("Pre-Black Friday," "Early Black Friday") needs Rakesh's sign-off before launch. "Back in stock" and scarcity strips only with a restock Rakesh confirms. *(stated · PETL112 brief, 2026-10-06; PETL92 hypothesis; docs/08)*
- Never put $35 corporate pricing, custom logos or engraving in consumer creative. Custom logos aren't a confirmed offering. *(stated · docs/01, docs/08)*

**Proof points**
- **Use "4.8 stars from over 1,400 reviews."** Retire "1,191 reviews" and "1,167." *(verified · on-site widget, 1,454 at 4.8, WPA 2026-10-27; newest source wins over docs/04 overlays)*
- **Hold every customer count.** "100,000+ customers" (homepage), "65,000+ units sold" (PDP) and "80,000 units in our first year" (founder, PRWeb 2026-09-14) contradict each other. "80,000+ customers" in older overlays is unsupported. If needed, use only "over 80,000 sold in our first year," attributed and dated. *(verified contradiction · WPA, BIA)*
- **"Sold out 7 times" is brand-level only,** never per colorway. Black sold out once. *(stated · client-endorsed, Fathom 2026-06-19; docs/01)*
- "World's first circular MagSafe-compatible power bank" is the brand's own line. Use it as their line, not as a fact a creator proves. Never "patented" (the homepage says both "patented" and "patent pending"); "patent-pending" only once Rakesh confirms a filing. *(verified contradiction · BIA, 2026-10-27)*
- "CE, FCC, RoHS, UL certified" is not a lead or safety claim until documents exist. "Military-grade magnets" and eco claims are out. "70% re-order in a second color" is out as a headline. *(stated · WPA; Grace rejected the re-order line, docs/01)*

**Fabricated content (the line that doesn't move)**
- No fabricated social proof presented as real customer comments in final production. Invented comments are layout placeholders only, clearly marked. Use real comments from Facebook or the PDP, first name plus last initial. *(stated · Grace, docs/04 §H)*
- No fake branded AI interface screenshots (ChatGPT, Google, Gemini UI recommending PETL). Real screen recordings are fine. A generic chat layout not styled as a brand is fine. *(stated · docs/04 §H, citing Meta and FTC risk)*
- Testimonial statics with a name, photo, age or stars must use real reviews. *(stated · docs/04 §H)*
- Don't present CJ or Claire as independent "Expert Tech Reviewer" press. They're PETL's own creators. *(inferred from ad names and Slack · BIA)*

## Do's and don'ts

**Brand feel, from Rakesh**
- "I want to keep the standard in ads, so it feels branded. I want to keep the brand clean and elegant." Ads that failed looked "gimmicky." "Creators need to feel luxury." *(stated · onboarding form 2026-01-12; onboarding call notes 2026-01-14)*
- "Double down on that it's circular - fits in your palm - not clunky." *(stated · Rakesh, onboarding call, 2026-01-14)* But circular is a supporting USP, not the problem. A whole concept on circular vs rectangular was "a waste of creative." *(stated · Grace, docs/04 §A)*
- **Real UGC for testimonial angles, not AI.** "We need to do this angle with real UGC not AI." AI VO over B-roll is accepted for top-of-funnel message tests. *(stated · Rakesh, Slack 2026-09-10; Fathom 2026-10-09)*

**PETL 2 Plus**
- **Never make the original PETL look weak.** Upgrade framing only: "The PETL you love, upgraded." Plus is more battery for heavy users. Mention big phones (Pro Max) once at most. Space Black is Plus-only. *(stated · PETL124 brief; docs/04 §E; docs/01)*

**Apple**
- **Apple as a foil or as praise works; Apple as an enemy doesn't.** "Feels like something Apple would make," "the MagSafe battery pack Apple should have made," "Apple discontinued theirs." Direct "US VS APPLE" confrontation got 0 purchases on $185. *(stated · docs/03, client dashboard ~2026-07-15)* The honesty disarm works: "I'm not saying it's an Apple product. It's not. It's compatible with Apple products." *(stated · docs/04 §E)*
- Competitors may be named: Apple, Anker, Belkin, Mophie. **Always concede Anker's capacity** ("It holds a lot of charge, I'll give it that"). Never put PETL's mAh next to bigger banks. Anker carries no loyalty tax, so it can be compared hard. *(stated · PETL48 hypothesis; docs/01. The explicit approval message was never located; scope data-limited)*

**Creators and filming**
- Creators film raw: no overlays or filters, vertical 1080x1920 or better, no branded clothing, cinematic mode preferred, emphasize the magnetic snap sound. *(stated · PETL68 brief)*
- Change location every 2 to 3 lines. Exception: a seated tech-reviewer unboxing. *(stated · docs/03, docs/04 §E)*
- Profanity soft or censored only ("freaking"). *(stated · Grace, Slack 2026-10-16)*
- Show real faces struggling with the exact problem. Never phone-screen-only or device-only hook shots. Every Apple mention cuts in 3 to 5 seconds of real Apple pack B-roll. Music: warm chill lofi, no swell at the reveal. Larger captions for 55+. *(stated · docs/04 §F)*
- Keep product needs minimal for the shipped creator roster (each got 1 Original + 1 PETL 2 Plus). That's a roster-logistics rule, not a creative rule. Concepts that need several units are fine when a creator already has them, like Aimee Swanson (5–6 PETLs) or the client's own creators. *(stated · CLAUDE.md export; scope clarified by Grace, 2026-10-27)*
- Creator casting: diverse in age and ethnicity, 20s to 30s and 40s to 60s+, both genders, iPhone Duo owners preferred, every creator shoots family gifting B-roll. *(stated · Pat, Slack internal 2026-09-29)*
- Who is on screen decides who Meta delivers to. Pick the creator for the audience you want. *(stated · Round 3 kickoff, 2026-04-22; docs/03)*

**Statics**
- Tabloid headline register (Daily Mail, Mirror): concrete nouns, real numbers, checkable claims, 5 to 7 words. *(stated · Grace, docs/04 §G)*
- Every variation must look visually different and carry different copy (Meta Andromeda). Don't recycle setups (plain front-on PETL, PETL-on-iPhone hero). *(stated · docs/04 §G)*
- Feature-led beats color-led. Acquisition statics carry no price point; BOFU statics carry the offer, ideally tied to an occasion and themed end to end. One trust badge per static. *(stated · docs/04 §G; docs/03)*
- Never brief receipt-style graphics, list-style visuals (packing lists, handwritten lists) or price-sticker gimmicks. *(stated · docs/04 §G)*
- Discount in creative for BOFU, not TOF. *(stated · Round 3 kickoff brainstorm, 2026-04-22)*

**Operational**
- INTL ad names must never carry "NSY." "This is important." *(stated · Rakesh, Slack 2026-06-10)* Use the pre-written ad titles from the dashboard so the feedback loop can learn. *(stated · Slack 2026-10-05)*
- When pricing or tiers shift, flag live creative with stale claims to Charley or Rakesh. It isn't an editor task. Known stale live ads: PETL65 ("free shipping on two or more"), the Father's Day winner ($49), PETL45 ($49, "half the price"), PETL53 ("Free shipping on 2+"). *(stated · docs/01, docs/08)*
- No Christmas creative until the client says so. For Batch 8, Christmas is parked: focus on Black Friday, Cyber Monday and general holiday gifting. *(stated · docs/08; Grace, 2026-10-27)*
- **Rules apply to new ads only.** Don't edit or pull live ads when prices, offers or claim rules change (PETL60, PETL29 and the rest stay as they are). Every rule in this file governs new creative. *(stated · Grace, 2026-10-27)*
- **Obvious fiction is fine in AI animation.** A made-up office mistake or comic chaos story (the RYZE "we messed up our sale" style) is fine as an AI animation, because it's clearly not real. It's entertainment urgency, not a factual claim. *(stated · Grace, 2026-10-27)*

## What has worked and what has failed

**Worked** (newest first)
- **"Top 3 Chargers for Grandparents"** (PETL65 and the client's Sean_911 re-shoot) is the volume engine: about $405K of $1.66M 90-day spend at 1.62 to 1.71 ROAS, under the 2.2 goal. PETL65 V4: $209,170, 1.65 ROAS, 5.13% CTR, now paused. Rakesh on launch: "Looks like we found a new winner. P65. Killer ad." *(verified · Meta pull 2026-10-27; stated · Slack 2026-08-13)* *Inferred:* it's a volume engine, not an efficiency engine.
- **Bundle and offer-led statics are the efficiency leaders at scale:** AR717 V1 Bundle Discount 2.33 ROAS on $36,504; CLD922 2.07; AR717 Gifting 2.08. *(verified · Meta pull 2026-10-27)* Price and bundle hooks carry the lowest cost per result. *(stated · Rakesh analytics readout, 2026-10-05)*
- **Three-device ad set: 2.29x on 9.8% of spend, "winner starved, needs more ads."** *(stated · PETL Analytics brief, week of 2026-10-26)* Multi-device charging is the #1 spec; show all three devices early. *(stated · docs/03)*
- **Ranking and authority formats:** PETL48 Top 5 Ranked (Jared), PETL63 ChatGPT ranking. Concede a rival's strength, then win on three devices, form and carry. *(stated · docs/03)*
- **PETL84 long-copy "It's Official" announcement:** 2.04 to 2.15 ROAS, though all variants were flagged to pause for decline in the week ending 2026-10-18. *(verified · Meta; stated · PETL Analytics)*
- **Feature-focused hooks and product-demo style** win; corporate gifting was the only situational angle that performed last round. *(stated · Rakesh readout, 2026-10-05)*
- Winning UGC runs **90 to 120 seconds** with over half the runtime on product, proof and close. *(stated · Grace, docs/04 §E)*
- Historic: bundle/save BOFU 2.63x, Christmas/holiday 2.67x ("Christmas V3" static 3.19x), situational PAS 2.40x, "Apple discontinued theirs" PETL25 2.63x at $19.4K in May. Light sky blue #C7E0F4 background shows up in the best creatives. *(stated · docs/03, lifetime export to 2026-04-17 and Round 4 data; older price points, not directly comparable)*

**Failed**
- **Lifestyle creative, social-proof hooks, transformation and decluttering angles, clever or vague lines.** "The gift that doesn't end up in a drawer" (PETL72) got 0 purchases recently. 25-34 targeting lost. *(stated · Rakesh readout, 2026-10-05; PETL Analytics weekly)*
- **High CTR is not efficiency.** PETL72, PETL125 and SEAN915 run 5%+ CTR with the worst CPAs. PETL125 "60 Years of Phones" soaks spend at about 1.32 to 1.35x. *(verified · Meta pull; stated · PETL Analytics daily 2026-10-26)*
- PETL112 dollar-off minimal price static ran 1.46x, "flagged-weak." PETL105 "New iPhone? PETL Fits" got lots of clicks that didn't convert, 1.33. *(stated · PETL Analytics; Charley, Slack 2026-10-20)*
- Scarcity-only "LAST CHANCE" 0.57x; aesthetics-only "BLUSH & GOLD RESTOCK" 1 purchase on $155; iPhone-model-specific hooks tested poorly. *(stated · client dashboard ~2026-07-15, docs/03)*
- Mother's Day 1.44, vague low-battery anxiety 1.29, ASMR 1.32, cables-only 1.37, generic travel 1.68. *(stated · Round 3 kickoff, 2026-04-22)*
- **Never run traffic, follower or Instagram-post boosts:** about $22K spent at 0.04 to 0.30x. The awareness campaign was killed by the client on 2026-09-11. *(stated · docs/03; Slack)*
- When iterating a winner, **change one variable at a time.** The Apple-discontinued female iteration underperformed because the script changed too. *(stated · docs/03)*

## Positioning and audience nuances

- **The real buyer is older than the brand's self-picture.** The onboarding form describes a design-conscious minimalist who likes "premium, understated brands." The account says core buyers are 55+, skewing female 65+, and Rakesh's own read is "older demographics work better." Default older-audience scripts to grandma and women; male 65+ is the most efficient and underweighted. *(stated · onboarding form 2026-01-12; CLAUDE.md export; Fathom 2026-10-09)* Rakesh still wants younger, college and Spanish-speaking tests. *(stated · Fathom 2026-10-09, diarization unreliable)*
- **Gifting is a core lane:** wife to husband, kids to parents, grandchild to grandparent, team and corporate. Gift buying starts about two months before an occasion. Gifting hooks stay evergreen ("a birthday or a holiday coming up") unless the round is seasonal. *(stated · Rakesh via Notion briefs; docs/04 §C)*
- **PETL can't win a spec-per-dollar fight.** It sits in the top fifth of category price with the smallest capacity. It wins on shape and carry: "the one you actually carry." Frame the original as the daily top-up you carry, not a brick replacement. *(inferred · external research 2026-10-27; stated · docs/01)*
- **Price is now the main objection** since the move from about $45 to $49 up to $64.95. The survey that named price first predates the move. *(stated · Notion hypotheses PETL96 on; inferred that it's larger now)*
- **The original vs Plus lead is unsettled.** PETL 2 Plus took 71.9% of revenue and 64.9% of units Sep 1 to 10, but a standing writing rule calls the original the volume seller people re-buy. Single-unit orders lost money while multi-unit tiers made margin. *(stated · Charley, Slack 2026-09-11; open loop in WPA)*
- **The 30-day trial is the strongest unspent card.** Few rivals advertise one and almost no live PETL copy mentions it. Grace wants risk reversal in every video from Round 5 on. *(inferred · WPA; stated · docs/01)*
- Space Black buyers run 91% men; multi-color ads lean to women 45-54. Colors seem to change who buys. *(stated · PETL Analytics, week of 2026-10-26)*
- US only for ads. Not sold on Amazon ("Thank God we don't sell over there"). *(stated · onboarding form; Rakesh, Fathom 2026-10-09)*

## Named preferences and standing instructions

**Grace (NSY Senior Creative Strategist)**
- **She's in the driver's seat.** Don't change what she didn't ask to change ("Additions mean additions"). If she asks only to add ideas to the Ideas Library, don't write them. *(stated · CLAUDE.md export)*
- **Show the script in chat first, get her explicit OK, then push to Notion.** Every brief goes through the `nsy-brief-builder` skill in the Official NSY Brief Template, exactly. Add the Time Log row after every push. *(stated · CLAUDE.md export; docs/05)*
- **Short, human, concise.** Scannable bullets, one-sentence concept descriptions, no essays. "Just the script" means only the script. One brief at a time unless she says batch. *(stated · CLAUDE.md export)*
- **10/10 or it isn't done.** A 4/10 means rebuild, not polish. Read the specs before ideating; accuracy beats cleverness. *(stated · docs/04 §A)*
- **Be honest when something is wrong, including your own mistakes.** When auditing, audit every column, not just one. *(stated · CLAUDE.md export)*
- **The hook is about 80% of the concept.** Four entry points per concept: moment or story, indignity, frequency or authority, direct-address callout. Name iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods for older audiences. *(stated · docs/04 §C)*
- Ask whether a brief is creator or editor-only; don't assume. Caption direction, footage and music come from Grace; mark STRATEGIST TO CONFIRM if missing. *(stated · docs/05)*
- **Run the Dojo checklist each batch:** top 10 ads reviewed for the shared pattern, one close rip from PETL's own niche, one adjacent or same-ICP rip, one untried format, one authority-led concept, one real-customer or follow-my-journey concept, max one AI format, landing page and tracking checked for TOF. Swipe winners closely. When the client pushes a format that isn't working, answer with three months of numbers split by format and by who's on screen. *(stated · Dojo Playbook, Grace, 2026-10-25)*
- Don't re-pitch rejected ideas, and don't remind Grace about the "iconic travel destinations" static. *(stated · docs/08)*
- Uses PETL herself at bedtime instead of the regular charger. *(stated · Fathom 2026-10-09)*

**Rakesh (founder and CEO, sole approver)**
- Final say on product, pricing and compliance calls. *(stated · CLAUDE.md export)*
- Expects his strategy followed: "I want you to follow this strategy for PETL." His PETL Analytics weekly brief is the guide for what to make next. *(stated · Slack 2026-09-28; docs/03)*
- Wants diversification, not reliance on one or two ads: no single ad over 25% of spend, 6+ concepts each at 3%+ of spend, 3 to 6 genuinely different new concepts a week, "Fewer, more different ideas." *(stated · PETL Analytics weekly)*
- Wants winners re-run with different faces to beat fatigue. Recommended tests: 60s-70s male "one device charges all," product demo plus 30% badge for female 65+, grandparent-as-recipient gift UGC, "3 chargers vs 1" for male 55-64, older couple buying two, age-appropriate female unboxing with all three devices charging in the first 3 seconds. *(stated · Rakesh readout, 2026-10-05)*
- Not keen to appear on camera; for a founder story, prefers AI VO or a narrator. *(stated · Fathom 2026-05-06)*
- Wants ads for the goPETL app and battery widget. Grace's rule: mention the app briefly as an extra USP, never a whole concept. Never imply remote monitoring of someone else's battery. *(stated · Rakesh, Slack 2026-07-29; docs/01)*

**Charley (NSY Growth Strategist, Meta)**
