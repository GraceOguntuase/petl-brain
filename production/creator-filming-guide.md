# PETL filming guide for creators

## Read this first

1. Do the NSY filming training linked in your brief. The brief says it plainly: "If the content is not in alignment with what is said here, we may ask for a reshoot" *(stated · Notion PETL93 brief, Oct 2026)*.
2. Then read this page. It covers the PETL-specific stuff the training can't.
3. Read your whole brief before you film, including the Shot List at the bottom. Most misses so far were skipped hooks or skipped shots, not bad acting (see "Problems we've seen").

**The ten rules that matter most**

- Film vertical, 1080x1920 or better, in good light. Cinematic mode if your iPhone has it.
- Lapel or headphone mic for anything with talking. No echoey or noisy rooms.
- Upload RAW. No text, no filters, no music.
- No branded or graphic clothing.
- No Apple logos on screen. Say "MagSafe-compatible", never "Apple-approved", "Made for Apple" or "official Apple".
- Always say "PETL 2 Plus" in full. It's pronounced "petal".
- Never say the original PETL gives a "full charge".
- No shots of PETL being used during a flight. Airport and destination are fine.
- Film every hook as its own take, 4 or 5 takes each. Film every shot on the Shot List.
- Name every file to match the shot name ("Shot 07 - Snap on at the counter") and upload to the Drive link in your brief.

*(Rules 1–4 stated · NSY standard shooting instructions, doc 05 and PETL93 brief. Rules 5–8 stated · compliance block, doc 05; N-MCD PETL68; external research 2026-10. Rules 9–10 stated · PETL93 "How to Read This Brief".)*

## 1. Handling the product on camera

PETL is a small round aluminum-and-glass disc that snaps onto the back of an iPhone 12 or newer with magnets, and can also charge AirPods and Apple Watch *(stated · website copy, Jan 2026)*. The client wants it to feel premium: "Creators need to feel luxury" and ads that failed "looked gimmicky" *(stated · Rakesh via Grace's onboarding notes, 2026-01-14)*.

**Before you start**
- Peel the protective film off the magnetic side (unless your list has a "film peel" shot) *(inferred from round 2 shot list, Feb 2026)*.
- Wipe the glass top. It shows fingerprints and smudges up close *(inferred)*.
- Know which unit is which. Round 7/8 creators got one original PETL and one PETL 2 Plus, in different colours *(stated · digest, Oct 2026)*. If the brief names one, use that one. The 2 Plus is slightly thicker.
- Use an iPhone 12 or newer. A case is fine and actually helps (see Apple logos below).

**The snap-on (the money shot)**
- This is the moment every edit leans on. Land it clearly on the first snap-on in the body *(stated · PETL93 editor notes, Oct 2026)*.
- Side profile of the iPhone, bring PETL in slowly, let the magnets pull it the last bit. Tilt the phone a little so the PETL logo reads *(stated · Grace to Jan, Slack, 2026-03-09)*.
- Film it from more than one angle *(stated · Caitlin shot list, Jan 2026: "CRITICAL: capture this from multiple angles")*.
- Get the click on the mic. "Emphasise the magnetic snap sound" *(stated · N-MCD PETL68)*. Keep the mic close and the room quiet.
- Say "snap it on", not "stick it on" *(stated · doc 04 word choices)*.
- Make the snap feel expensive: weight in the hand, a slow close-up of the aluminum edge, a clean sound. Cheap $10 power banks own the plain snap-on video on TikTok, so ours has to look and sound better *(inferred · external research 2026-10)*.

**The charging animation**
- Front view of the iPhone screen right after the snap, so the circular charging animation and battery % show *(stated · round 2 shot list, Feb 2026)*.
- Turn screen brightness up and hold the shot 2–3 seconds after the animation, so the editor has room *(inferred)*.
- Don't fake a dead phone with a screenshot. If the brief wants "1%" or "5%", let the phone actually run low, or tell us and we'll handle it in the edit *(inferred)*.

**The battery widget and the app**
- PETL shows up in the iPhone's own battery widget, no app needed *(stated · website copy)*. Swipe to the widget screen with PETL attached and hold so the PETL row is readable.
- The free goPETL app shows Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, live wattage and time-to-full *(stated · website copy / PETL APP INFO, Aug 2026)*. We have no app footage at all yet *(stated · Grace asked Rakesh for some, Slack, 2026-08-31; none found since)*. If you have the app, a screen recording of the Lock Screen while charging is gold. Ask your NSY contact first.

**Watch and AirPods on the disc**
- Set PETL flat on a table, mid-frame. Place the Apple Watch on top until it starts charging, then do the same with the AirPods case *(stated · round 2 shot list, Feb 2026)*. Get the screen or light that shows it's charging.
- You need to own an Apple Watch and AirPods for these shots. One Round 6 creator couldn't film them because she had no Watch *(stated · Alyanna, Slack, 2026-09-22)*.
- Don't explain how the Watch charges in your own words. Say only what the script says. How the Watch charges is still being confirmed with the client *(stated · digest open question 4, Oct 2026)*.
- Never say all three charge "at the same time" unless the script says it *(stated · doc 08, open flag)*.

**Other product moments worth having**
- StandBy: iPhone on its side, propped on PETL's flat edge, StandBy screen showing *(stated · Caitlin shot list, Jan 2026)*.
- Size: PETL in the palm, in a coin pocket, next to keys or a wallet. Approved phrases: "almost the size of a golf ball", "smaller than your palm", "fits in your coin pocket" *(stated · doc 04)*.
- The LED ring that shows battery level *(stated · website copy)*.

**What creators should NOT film for the shot list:** product macros, rotations or unboxings. "We have a ton of those." The one exception is new product (golds, PETL 2 Plus, the new colours launching 2026-11-04), where the list will ask for it *(stated · doc 05, Grace)*. The snap-on, charging and three-device moments still belong in your script takes, because they need you in frame.

## 2. Shot lists by format

These are the formats the team actually runs. Your brief's Shot List always wins over this table. Shot lists are extra B-roll only, never repeats of what you film for the hooks and body *(stated · doc 05)*.

| Format | Examples | Where | Must-get shots | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Talking-head testimonial / gifting story** (older creator) | PETL65, PETL74, PETL75, PETL99 | Home, changing room every 2–3 lines | You living the problem (phone dying, reaching for a cable, hunting an outlet), the gift being handed over, first snap-on, all three devices, StandBy on the nightstand, PETL going into a pocket or bag | Stay on your face for the "after" part. Don't make it a phone-screen video *(stated · doc 04 E, F)* |
| **Ranking / Top 3 / Top 5 / comparison** | PETL48, PETL57, PETL63, PETL65, PETL97, PETL126 | Seated at a table, busy hands | Each product picked up as you rank it, the bulky brick in a pocket, PETL snap-on as #1, side-by-side size | Blur or turn away competitor logos. PETL gets 9/10 with a small flaw, or 10/10 only if the script says *(stated · doc 04 E; compliance block)* |
| **Yapper** | PETL102, PETL127 | Kitchen or parked car | You always doing something: cutting bright fruit (dragon fruit, pineapple), makeup in a parked car. About 90% talking, B-roll only around the reveal | Never a static head against a blank wall *(stated · doc 04 creator notes)* |
| **Couple / family gifting** | PETL73, PETL93, PETL95, PETL96, PETL126 | Your real home, real family | Real partner, kids, parents or grandparents on camera: the box handed over, their reaction, them snapping it on. | **Unboxing** (new product only) | PETL64 | Clean, colourful table | Box, lid lift, film peel, first snap, size next to the original. Rakesh wants an age-appropriate female version with all three devices charging in the first 3 seconds *(stated · doc 06, doc 08)* | Only for golds, PETL 2 Plus, new colours. | **Wordless snap-on demo** (organic test, not yet briefed) | none yet | Bright, real-life spot | Snap, click, charging animation, nothing else | The proven TikTok format, but owned by cheap banks, so it has to look premium *(stated · external research 2026-10; "not yet briefed" inferred)* |
| **Editor-only B-roll + AI VO** | PETL118–125 | No creator | Built from the library | Not a creator job. The shot list gets a red callout saying why *(stated · doc 05)* |

**Every Round 8+ creator also films:** family gifting B-roll (mom to husband; younger creators to parents or grandparents; older men to family), plus 2–3 shots of themselves using PETL for the library *(stated · Round 8 plan, 2026-09-29; doc 05)*.

**Shot list sizes:** 5–8 shots for a narrow concept, 15–20 for a new creator or broad concept. When one creator films two briefs, the shot list sits on the first brief only *(stated · doc 05)*.

**Product limits:** each creator gets one or two units. Never brief anything needing several units, like a five-PETL stack *(stated · doc 05)*.

## 3. Creator criteria by format

**Who buys.** Per Grace (2026-10-27), the buyer skews 45–75, women 65+ are the biggest group, and a big share is gifting to grandparents *(stated · Grace, task brief 2026-10-27)*. The account backs it: the F65+ segment was "our highest-volume buyer (426 purchases)" *(stated · Notion PETL65 hypothesis)*, PETL65 with a 65+ female creator is the #1 ad family *(verified · Meta pull 2026-10-27, via digest)*, and Rakesh says "older demographics work better" *(stated · bi-weekly 2026-10-09)*. Meta tends to show an ad to people who look like the creator: female creators skew delivery female, male skew male *(stated · Round 3 summary 2026-04-22)*. So the creator is also the targeting.

**Standing criteria for every creator** *(stated · Pat's summary, Slack #internal-petl, 2026-09-29, unless noted)*
- Mix of ages and ethnicities, "not exclusively Caucasian".
- Both genders, younger (20s–30s) and older (40s–60s+).
- Owns an iPhone 12 or newer. iPhone Duo owners preferred for Duo briefs.
- Can film family gifting B-roll with a real family member.
- Owns an Apple Watch and AirPods if the brief shows three devices *(inferred from the 2026-09-22 miss)*.
- Comfortable with soft language. If a script has a swear, the creator can swap a softer word like "freaking" *(stated · Grace approved for Jenni, Slack, 2026-10-16)*.

| Format | Creator to book | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Testimonial / gifting story | Woman 55–75, or man 55–65+. A real PETL owner is best for testimonials | F65+ is the biggest buyer; PETL74 cast "a genuine PETL owner rather than a hired creator" *(stated · Notion PETL74)*. M55–64 is efficient but older male content is "showing fatigue", so use new faces *(stated · PETL76 hypothesis; bi-weekly 2026-09-25)* |
| Ranking / comparison | Older woman for the grandparent version (PETL65), older man 50–65 for tech comparisons (PETL97), younger grandchild for PETL95 | Peer-to-peer beats expert authority for the 65+ buyer *(stated · PETL65 hypothesis)* |

| Couple / busy mom | Mom 38–48 with her real husband and kids | PETL93/96 *(stated · Grace's Round 7 rundown, Slack, 2026-10-01)*. Check the partner is around for the whole shoot; one creator's husband was away *(stated · Slack, 2026-10-09)* |
| Yapper | Woman 22–30, chatty and expressive | PETL98/102 *(stated · Round 7 rundown, 2026-10-01)* |

## 4. Audio and light

**Audio**
- Lapel or headphone mic, close to your mouth, off rubbing clothes *(stated · PETL93 shooting instructions)*.
- Film 10 seconds, play it back on headphones, then film the rest *(inferred, carried from NSY practice)*.
- For product shots, capture clean sound of: the box opening, the magnetic snap, fingers on the aluminum, the iPhone charge chime *(stated · round 1 and round 2 shot lists)*.
- No music in your raw files. Editors add calm lofi later *(stated · doc 04 F)*.
- If you're sick or your voice is off, say so before you film the script. Quinn asked to wait until he sounded normal (Slack, 2026-03-09), which was the right call *(stated)*.

**Light and background**
- Natural, soft, even light. Face the window. No harsh shadows, no dark rooms *(stated · round 1 shot list; PETL93)*.
- No plain white backgrounds. Grace asked for reshoots for this: "aesthetic & colorful, not plain white... If she can film outside, that would be even better" *(stated · Slack, 2026-03-09)*. Plants, a colourful desk, warm real rooms.
- For lived-in briefs (PETL93), don't tidy up. "Natural daylight, lived-in and relaxed" *(stated · PETL93)*.
- Tripod or gimbal for product shots *(stated · round 1 shot list)*.
- Change location every 2–3 lines on talking-head scripts (rooms in one home is fine). Seated ranking/unboxing is the exception *(stated · doc 04)*.

## 5. Compliance on camera

| Rule | What it means on set | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No Apple logos or marks | Keep the back of a bare iPhone out of frame. Use a case or film from the side. No Apple Store bags or boxes. Apple's own pack only appears as editor-sourced footage | *(stated · N-MCD PETL68 editor notes; "use a case" is inferred)* |
| Say "MagSafe-compatible" | Never "Apple-approved", "Apple-certified", "Made for Apple", "official Apple". "Compatible with iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods" is fine | *(stated · onboarding form 2026-01-12; compliance block doc 05)* |
| "PETL 2 Plus" in full | Not "PETL Plus", not "the Plus", not "PETL 2" | *(stated · N-MCD PETL124; doc 05)* |
| Never trash the original | When showing the 2 Plus, it's "the PETL you love, upgraded" | *(stated · doc 04 E)* |
| No "full charge" on the original | The 3,000mAh PETL gives about 55% of an iPhone 16 Pro over MagSafe. Say "top-up" or "about another ten hours of talk time". "Full extra charge" belongs to PETL 2 Plus only, and even that is about 92% on a 16 Pro | *(stated · PDP via external research 2026-10; doc 04 "about another ten hours")* |
| No in-flight use | No PETL charging a phone in a plane seat. Airport, gate and destination are fine. Southwest capped passengers at one power bank from 2026-04-20 and ICAO moved to ban in-flight recharging | *(stated · external research 2026-10, PIRG and TravelPulse coverage)*. The round 1 shot list asked for an in-flight shot (Jan 2026); don't film it now |
| No airline names or logos | No readable airline branding. Say "your boarding pass is on your phone" | *(stated · doc 08 rejected list)* |
| Blur or hide competitor logos | Turn old power banks logo-down. Editors blur the rest | *(stated · compliance block doc 05; PETL93)* |
| No Qi2 claim | Don't say Qi2 | *(stated · compliance block doc 05)* |
| Don't improvise prices or offers | Say only the offer in your script. Never "$49", "15% off", "free shipping on two", "half the price" | *(stated · compliance block doc 05)* |
| Say "Apple Watch" | Never "iWatch" | *(stated · doc 04)* |
| Call it what it is | "This little circular power bank", never "this thing" or "a circle" | *(stated · doc 04; N-MCD PETL104)* |
| No branded clothing | Plain clothes | *(stated · standard shooting instructions)* |

## 7. Before you upload

- [ ] Every hook filmed as its own take, 4–5 takes each.
- [ ] Every Shot List shot filmed and named to match.
- [ ] Played back every talking clip on headphones. Every word is clear.
- [ ] The snap click is audible on at least one take.
- [ ] Charging animation and battery % readable on screen.
- [ ] Good front light, colourful real background, no plain white wall.
- [ ] No Apple logo, airline logo, competitor logo or branded clothing in frame.
- [ ] Said "MagSafe-compatible" and "PETL 2 Plus" correctly. No "full charge" on the original. No in-flight shots.
- [ ] Raw files only. No text, filters or music.
- [ ] Uploaded to the Drive link in the brief.

Not sure? Ask your NSY contact before you film, not after.
