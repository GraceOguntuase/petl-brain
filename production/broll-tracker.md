## How to read this

- **delivered\*** = the creator's folder was posted as uploaded and approved or moved to editing, but nobody has logged each shot as checked. Check Drive before an edit relies on it, then drop the asterisk.
- **delivered** = a source names the shot itself as filmed.
- **missing** = a source says it wasn't filmed, or no footage exists.
- **unknown** = no source either way.
- Which brief a Round 7 creator filmed is sometimes inferred from Grace's casting rundown (Slack, 2026-10-01) and Alyanna's shortlists (2026-10-06). Those rows say *inferred*.
