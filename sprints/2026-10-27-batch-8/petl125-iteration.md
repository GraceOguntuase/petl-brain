# PETL125 iteration: same story, product by 20 seconds, then an us vs them

## Diagnosis

PETL125 is a `B-roll mashup + Voiceover` timeline for a problem-unaware audience. It spent $31,609 for $41,674 revenue over 30 days, 1.32 ROAS and $99.71 per purchase, well under the 2.2 goal (verified, Meta pull 2026-10-27). The story holds attention: 24.4% thruplays per impression is the highest of the account's top 35 spenders over 30 days, and outbound CTR is 3.57% (CTR all 5.09%). But the average watch is about 30 seconds, while PETL only appears around 1:40 into the live cut (stated · Grace), so most viewers leave before they ever see the product. This is a structural rescue at Grace's request, aimed at conversion: getting more sales out of a story that already earns attention.

**What stays:** the 5 hooks (module 01 and hooks 2–5), the nostalgic era looks, and the wall callback.

**What changes, two levers:** (1) pacing: the timeline gets compressed, and PETL arrives at about 18 to 20 seconds instead of 1:40; (2) a new `Us vs Them` comparison module in the solution half. To learn which lever did the work, run a control cut with the same new pacing and no comparison module. Watch and AirPods arrive at 26 to 32 seconds; brand-lens says to show all three devices early, so the editor can pull that beat forward if the cut allows.
