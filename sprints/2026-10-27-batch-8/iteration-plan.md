# Batch 8 iteration plan (checkpoint)

## Account read

PETL spent $653,851 in the 30 days to Oct 26 at 1.64 ROAS. The top of the account is four creative ideas: PETL60's apology video ($137.2K, 1.69), the Sean_911 / PETL65 "Top 3" family ($126.5K, 1.58, tiring), CLD_1001's three-band static ($53.6K at 1.79, almost all of it since Oct 20), and PETL61 "Meet PETL 2 Plus" ($23.7K, 1.96, rising). The rest of the top 10 is bundle and discount statics (CLD922, AR717) and the fading PETL84 long-copy static. Hook rate isn't readable through this connection, so video reads use thruplays per impression and average watch time (verified, Meta pull 2026-10-27).

## Selected ads, ranked

1. **CLD_1001, the three-band static (in-house).** High-riser: $382 on Oct 20 to $12.7–14.3K a day by Oct 24–26. It holds a $43–53 CPM, against $413 on PETL60, and Facebook feed takes 66% of its spend at 1.90. ROAS stepped down from 2.41 to about 1.6 when spend went up 5x, and 7-day frequency is 1.54. **Promo caveat:** the image carries "UP TO 30% OFF." The method says not to iterate on a promotional top spender. PETL's bundle ladder is standing pricing, not a time-boxed sale (verified, live PDP), so it's iterated here, but the first test isolates the badge (A3) so we learn whether the bands or the badge are doing the work. Grace named this ad.
2. **PETL61 V4, "Meet PETL 2 Plus" (NSY, Jared, 78s, live since Aug 14).** Slow-burner. ROAS went 1.79 to 2.18 week on week while Meta cut its spend 34%, with no fatigue (CPM $154 to $138, frequency 1.46). Its 7.7% thruplays per impression and 6–7s average watch on a 78s cut say the long body barely gets watched (verified), so a short cut is the obvious first test. It sells an upgrade to people who already know PETL.
3. **PETL60 V1, the apology ("To the 1,000 people who tried to buy a black PETL… we're sorry"), B-roll plus female AI VO, 68s.** The top spender. ROAS rose 1.62 to 1.89 as Meta moved spend to CLD_1001. CTR is softening (4.30% to 4.06%), with no ROAS fatigue. Its impressions are the most expensive in the account ($413 CPM). The "sold out" event is from July, so the claim is getting stale. This is where Grace's whisper idea fits best: same script, same B-roll, one variable.

## Not selected

- **Sean_911 H4 / PETL65 family:** flat at about 1.6 on $126.5K, and PETL65 V4 is paused. Big spend that's tiring needs a bigger swing than an iteration. The two-person tier list (PETL126) and the ranked static (PETL81) are already the swings.
- **CLD922 V1/V2 and AR717 V1/V3:** bundle and discount statics, the method's promotional case. CLD922 is improving with little spend; it can take a headline test later. AR717 V1 shows early fatigue and V3 is paused.
- **PETL84 V2/V3:** fatigued (2.04 to 1.56) or paused.

## Iterations, one variable each

### On CLD_1001 (A)
- **A1. Format swap to motion video (format, the loudest lever).** Same three bands, same colors, same headline. Each band becomes a 2–3 second clip of real hands: the PETL snaps onto an iPhone in a case (orange band), an Apple Watch is set on top (sage band), AirPods are set on top (pink band). One device per beat, which also keeps it clear of "at the same time." 4:5 for Facebook feed, about 10–12 seconds, no VO, the click sound only. Fastest to make, B-roll on hand.
- **A2. Frankenstein stitch.** A1's 4-second three-band open as the hook, then PETL61's proven body. Two validated parts, one new ad. PETL61's weak watch-through makes a stronger first 3 seconds the right fix.
- **A3. Badge out.** The same static without "UP TO 30% OFF." It tells us whether the message or the offer carries it, and gives an evergreen version for after Black Friday.
- **A4. Static mashup with a winning static.** Add a fourth band with CLD922's bundle line (the 3-pack per-unit price, once the BF price is confirmed) or the 4.8-star review count. Combines two winners' strongest elements.
- **A5. Headline style swap.** Same bands, the headline changes from a benefit ("One Charger. Three Devices.") to a customer line: "Freedom from Cords" (survey #GP5207) or "Not having to deal with so many wires." (#GP4464). Same message, now in her words.
- **A6. Colorway swap for the Nov 4 launch.** Burgundy, Glacier and Silver as the three bands. One visual variable, timed to a real event.
- **A7. Space Black version for men.** Dark bands, everything else the same. Men are 36.6% of this ad's spend at 1.70, and Space Black buyers run 91% men (stated, PETL Analytics). Persona is the last lever, so it's ranked last.

### On PETL61 (B)
- **B1. Short cut.** 78s down to about 30s. Keep the hook and the first proof beat, end on the offer. Viewers average 6–7 seconds, so most never reach the close.
- **B2. Same script, new creator.** The R8 brief already asked for "a new face (not Jared)." First pick Rod Kynaston, backup Bo Richardson. Same age band, fresh face.
- **B3. Hook headline banner.** Add CLD_1001's "One Charger. Three Devices." as a bold top banner over the first 3 seconds. Very low effort.

### On PETL60 (C)
- **C1. Whisper VO.** Same script and B-roll, with the female AI voice re-read as a whisper. One variable, the move from the Dojo story. Deploy-fast.
- **C2. Event swap.** Same structure: "To everyone who waited for Burgundy… we're sorry." Only if Burgundy truly sold out or had a waitlist. This replaces PETL145 as a net-new slot, since it's really an iteration.
- **C3. Short cut.** 68s to about 30s. Thruplays per impression are 17.7%, the best in the account, so test whether the short version keeps the hold.
- **C4. Spanish VO.** Same B-roll, Spanish voiceover. This is the existing PETL141, which is an iteration, not net new.

## Proposed batch split

Today's draft is 35 net new. Grace wants iterations to be most of the work. The account has three iteratable winners right now, so 80% (28 iterations) would mean about 9 per ad and would start repeating itself. Proposal: **21 iterations and 14 net new (60%)**.
- **Videos (20):** 9 iterations (A1, A2, B1, B2, B3, C1, C2, C3, C4) plus 11 net new.
- **Statics (15):** 12 iterations (A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, plus static ports of PETL61 and PETL60, plus headline swaps on CLD922 and the apology static) plus 3 net new.
- Net new videos drop from 20 to 11. Keep the strongest UGC: PETL128 to 131, 132, 133, 135, 138, 140, 142, and one non-UGC format (PETL143 call-in or PETL144 grading).
