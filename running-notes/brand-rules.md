# Brand rules — PETL

The brand's own definitions of winning and how the account is organized. Everything here is **stated** input, authoritative for intent, with who said it and when. A rule that conflicts with the data gets surfaced, not silently obeyed or dropped.

## How this brand defines winning

- **Primary campaign objective:** Sales (purchases). US only for ads: "Target Audience is US. the rest we let META handle." *(stated · onboarding form, 2026-01-12)*
- **North-star targets, current:** **Meta ROAS 2.2x** and **Shopify blended MER 2.4x**. *(stated · PETL Analytics goals, weekly briefs from 2026-09-28)*
- **The profitability floor:** "For us to be profitable we need 2.4." *(stated · Rakesh, Slack 2026-07-27)* On blended, "anything above, like, 2.1 is okay." *(stated · Charley paraphrasing Rakesh, Fathom 2026-10-09)*
- **Spend and revenue goals:** September revenue about $1.1M; October target about $1.2M to $1.3M; daily spend cap about $24K in October and about $31K in November. *(stated · Rakesh, Fathom 2026-10-09 transcript, diarization unreliable)*
- **Diversification is part of winning** (Andromeda rules in PETL Analytics): no single ad over 25% of spend; 6+ concepts each at 3%+ of spend; 3 to 6 genuinely different new concepts a week; "Fewer, more different ideas." *(stated · PETL Analytics weekly)*
- **Creator hit rate** is a graded metric, split by source (NSY in-house, Sean, CLD, AR, RoG, Catalogue). *(stated · PETL Analytics weekly, week ending 2026-10-18)*
- **CPA read:** account CPA baseline roughly $50 to $67; under $50 CPA is a genuine winner. *(stated · docs/03, 14-day snapshot 2026-08-26)* The 90-day account CPA is now $69.15, so that bar has drifted. *(verified · Meta pull 2026-10-27; drift inferred)*
- **Secondary metrics they still weigh:** CTR, hook rate and CVR from Rakesh's dashboard; AOV and units per order (the bundle job). Hook rate isn't reproducible from the Meta pull; 2-second plays returned null. *(stated · PETL Analytics; data-limited · digest §6)*
- **When two ads diverge on spend:** no verbatim rule on file. Practice says efficiency counts as well as spend. Rakesh pushed to pause PETL61 while it was the top spender ("this is eating ad budget. ROAS is not great"), and PETL Analytics flags budget soaks like PETL125 as WATCH rather than winners. *(stated · Slack 2026-09-03; PETL Analytics daily 2026-10-26; rule inferred)*
- **Kill rule in the account:** under $300 spend in 7 days, or under 1.9x Meta ROAS. *(stated · Charley, Slack 2026-07-17; may be stale after the October consolidation)*
- **Where performance gets read:** Rakesh's own PETL Analytics platform (Shopify plus Meta), alongside in-platform Meta. Meta-reported ROAS runs about 0.3 to 0.4 below Shopify blended (1.54 vs 1.89 on 7 days, 2026-10-20). An incremental read, total spend against total revenue, is also used. *(stated · Charley, Slack 2026-10-20; Fathom 2026-05-06)* No Northbeam or Triple Whale on file. *(data-limited)*

**Where it stands now:** 90-day Meta ROAS 1.69 on $1.66M spend; week of 2026-10-26 the account ran 1.66x Meta; daily 10-26 ROAS7 1.73x, MER7 2.11x. Below both targets, with a weekend spike to 2.2x blended on 2026-10-24 to 25. *(verified · Meta pull 2026-10-27; stated · PETL Analytics; Charley 2026-10-26)*

## How the account is organized

- **Ad account:** Meta 3803347073290974, "gopetl's ad account," USD. *(verified · Meta Ads MCP, 2026-10-27)*
- **Ad naming convention:** `SOURCE | CODE | VARIANT | TITLE`. SOURCE is `NSY` for agency ads or `INTL` for in-house; CODE is the Notion concept code (`PETL65`) or the in-house creator-and-date code (`Sean_911`, `AR717`, `CLD922`, `ROG813`); VARIANT is `V1` to `V4` or a hook number `H1` to `H4`; TITLE is the concept name in caps. Example: `NSY | PETL65 | V4 | TOP 3 CHARGERS FOR GRANDPARENTS`. Older ads use a date prefix (`May 1 | PETL 32 AR | V4 | 30 P`). *(verified pattern · Meta top-30 table, digest §6)*
- **INTL ad names must never carry "NSY."** Use the pre-written ad titles from the dashboard so the feedback loop can learn. *(stated · Rakesh, Slack 2026-06-10; Slack 2026-10-05)*
- **Ad sets:** consolidated to about 7 with flexible ads in mid-October: PRODUCT FOCUS, TOP PERFORMERS, BUNDLES DISCOUNTS, GIFTING, Testimonial, PETL PLUS, THREE DEVICES (plus legacy sets like US VS THEM). *(stated · Slack 2026-10-15 to 10-26; PETL Analytics week of 2026-10-26)*
