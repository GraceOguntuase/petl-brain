## Rakesh's analytics and how he grades NSY

- Rakesh runs his own platform, **PETL Analytics (analytics-petl.com)**: P&L, forecast, Shopify, Meta Performance tabs, an "Andromeda score," and creator hit rates. It sends automated weekly and daily briefs from sales@gopetl.com to Grace and Charley; weekly briefs started around 2026-09-28. *(stated · digest §1, §6)*
- **What it grades on:**
  - Meta ROAS against 2.2x and Shopify MER against 2.4x, with a weekly "$X a week short at this spend" gap per ad set. *(stated · PETL Analytics)*
  - **Creator hit rate by source.** 28 days to week ending 2026-10-18: NSY in-house $308,343 at 1.59x with a **10% hit rate (3 of 29)**; Sean $153,443 at 1.60x; CLD $75,778 at 1.88x, 16% hit rate; AR $32,043 at 1.81x; RoG $13,495 at 1.61x. NSY's hit rate also trails the account's 14%. *(stated · PETL Analytics weekly)*
  - **Andromeda diversification rules:** no single ad over 25% of spend, 6+ concepts at 3%+ of spend each, 3 to 6 genuinely different new concepts a week. Score 69/100 week of 10-18; 63 on 10-26 with top-ad share up to 27%. *(stated · PETL Analytics)*
  - Per-ad PAUSE and WATCH calls (e.g., PAUSE on PETL25 H3, PETL84 V1 to V3, PETL72 V3; WATCH on PETL125 as a budget soak). *(stated · PETL Analytics weekly, week ending 2026-10-18)*
  - Direction for the next round, e.g. 10-26: "Direct the next brief round into Gifting and Proof and comparison … with a genuinely different buyer/scene, not another Three-device variant." *(stated · PETL Analytics daily, 2026-10-26)*
- **Credit is contested.** Rakesh on 2026-10-02: "We are taking you over. Last 7 days, 75% of sales from INTL ads." There is no shared definition of credit between in-house and NSY ads. *(stated · Slack client; inferred gap)*
- He expects his steer to be followed: "I want you to follow this strategy for PETL." *(stated · Slack 2026-09-28)*
