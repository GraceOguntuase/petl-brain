# Success definition — PETL

## The main business objective right now

**Scale profitably into Q4 on Meta: hit 2.2x Meta ROAS and 2.4x Shopify MER while spend climbs, and stop leaning on one or two ads.**

The pieces, in the client's words and numbers:
- **Profit floor:** "For us to be profitable we need 2.4." *(stated · Rakesh, Slack 2026-07-27)* Current goals in his dashboard are **Meta ROAS 2.2x and Shopify MER 2.4x**; on blended, "anything above, like, 2.1 is okay." *(stated · PETL Analytics; Charley paraphrasing Rakesh, Fathom 2026-10-09)*
- **Scale:** September revenue about $1.1M, October target about $1.2M to $1.3M, daily spend cap about $24K in October rising to about $31K in November. *(stated · Rakesh, Fathom 2026-10-09)*
- **Diversification:** "Currently we are only relying on 1-2 creatives … I want us to be more diversified." *(stated · Rakesh, Slack 2026-09-28)* His dashboard grades it: no ad over 25% of spend, 6+ concepts at 3%+ each, 3 to 6 genuinely different new concepts a week. *(stated · PETL Analytics)*
- **The Q4 calendar:** PETL 2 Plus Burgundy, Glacier and Silver on 2026-11-04, Black Friday 2026-11-27, Cyber Monday 2026-11-30, Christmas ground cut-off 2026-12-18. *(stated · PETL Analytics weekly, week of 2026-10-26)*
- **For NSY specifically:** win a bigger share of sales back from the in-house team, which Rakesh says drove 75% of sales in one 7-day window in early October. *(stated · Slack 2026-10-02; docs/03 "the goal is for NSY to take a bigger share")*

## What that means for the work

*Parker's inference.* This is an efficiency-at-scale goal, not a pure scale goal. Spend is rising into Q4 while CPMs have roughly tripled since May ($53 to about $140), so a concept only counts as a win if it holds near 2.2x Meta at real spend, not if it wins clicks. High-CTR, low-efficiency ads (PETL125, PETL72) read as soaks, not wins. Rank recommendations by three things together: efficiency at scale, whether the idea is genuinely different from what's already carrying spend (Rakesh grades diversity directly), and whether it pushes multi-unit orders, since single-unit orders lost money in September while 2-packs and up made margin. Bundle and offer-led creative, the starved three-device set, gifting and proof/comparison are where the account currently says efficiency lives. Every recommendation should also be judged by how it moves NSY's hit rate in Rakesh's dashboard, because that's the scorecard the relationship is read on.
