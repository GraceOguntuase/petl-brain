- **Certifications:** which documents exist for CE, FCC, RoHS, UL and WEEE, especially UL? *(stated · BIA)*
- **MagSafe licensing:** is there any MFi or MagSafe license? The PDP uses "MagSafe®" and the homepage says "Made for iPhone Pro & Pro Max," both close to the onboarding ban. *(verified · BIA)*
- **PETL 2 Plus spec sheet:** "up to 15 extra hours" (briefs) vs "10+ Extra hours" (live PDP); confirm capacity, output, weight and airline status in writing. *(verified conflict · WPA)*
