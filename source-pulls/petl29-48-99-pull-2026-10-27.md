## 2. PETL48 — Top 5 MagSafe Power Banks Ranked (Authority Review)

### Hooks (verbatim)
- **H1 (lead):** "I spent over $1,000 on every best-selling MagSafe power bank I could find. Here are the top 5, ranked from worst to best, so you don't waste your money." (desk with PETL + Apple pack; montage of other 3 on "$1,000+ tested")
- **H2:** "What's the best MagSafe power bank you can buy in 2026? I tested every top-selling one - here are my top 5 ranked." (search-intent framing; Google-search style cut "Best MagSafe Power Bank 2026," then fast flip through dozens of power banks)
- **H3:** "I've tested over 50 power banks this year - Apple, Anker, Belkin, Mophie, and one you've probably never heard of. Here's my ranking from worst to best." (quick cuts per brand, ends on PETL in hand)
- **H4:** "There are thousands of MagSafe power banks online - I tested the top 5 best-selling ones so you don't have to. Here's how they ranked." (Amazon listing scroll down to 5 finalists)

### Body structure (same across hooks)
Countdown with on-screen ranking cards:
1. **#5 Apple MagSafe Battery Pack, 5/10:** $99, iPhone only, 1,460mAh "barely half a charge," discontinued 2023.
2. **#4 Mophie Snap+, 7/10:** $50, 5,000mAh, only 7.5W (half of MagSafe), iPhone only. "Slow, and it's limited."
3. **#3 Belkin BoostCharge Pro, 7.5/10:** same 5,000mAh, full 15W, "solid pick," but rectangular brick, iPhone only.
4. **#2 Anker MagGo, 8.5/10 (credibility beat):** 10,000mAh, highest capacity, Qi2 15W, smart display; but rectangular, awkward in pocket, $79, iPhone only.
5. **#1 PETL, 9/10 (reveal):** $49, half the Anker price; world's first circular MagSafe, fits flat in pocket; machined aluminium + tempered glass; doubles as nightstand stand; ~10 hours extra battery; "real reason it beat the Anker" is it charges iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods; honest concession "not the biggest battery... but the one you'll actually carry"; 9 not 10 because "I wish it came in more colours... I want it in gold."
6. **CTA:** "If you want to grab one for yourself, head to gopetl.com. You can get 2 for 20% off, or 5 for 30% off - perfect if you've got a few Apple people in the family. Plus free shipping on any order of 2 or more." End card: gopetl.com · 2+ = 20% OFF · 5+ = 30% OFF · FREE SHIPPING ON 2+.
