## 4. Brand rules, do's and don'ts, claims, compliance

**Compliance (client-stated, onboarding — the only written restrictions):**
- "❌ Apple Affiliation or Endorsement Claims. Do not imply or state: • "Apple-approved" • "Apple-certified" • "Made for Apple" (unless officially licensed) • "Official Apple accessory". You may say "compatible with iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods" only in a factual compatibility context." (*Stated — N-OB, 2026-01-12.*)
- NSY extension in briefs: never use "Apple-approved / Apple-certified / Made for Apple / Apple-made / Apple-endorsed" in captions, overlays or VO; **"MagSafe-compatible" is the correct phrasing; no Apple logos or brand marks** (*stated — N-MCD PETL68 editor notes*). PETL51: "Brand names in text only, no logos."
- Competitor naming **allowed** (Apple, Anker, Belkin, Mophie) since ~late May 2026 (*stated — N-MCD PETL48/57 hypothesis*). Blurred competitors used before that (PETL41).
- **Note the brand's own website calls it "MagSafe®"** ("PETL MagSafe® Compatible Battery Pack") — Apple's trademark used with ® on a third-party product (*verified — D-CTX website copy*). *Inferred:* creative should stick to "MagSafe-compatible" and avoid "MagSafe battery pack" as a product noun; flag to Rakesh rather than assume.

**Pricing/offer accuracy rules:**
- Never use PETL15, "15% off," "Free shipping on 2+," or "$49" anywhere (N-MCD PETL68, 2026-09-08). Free shipping = $129 cart value. Match ads to the live PDP offer — Rakesh: "please check our prices and offer have changed, make sure new ads match the present offer" (S-client 2026-08-25). The "half the price of Apple's $99 pack" line no longer holds on one unit; compare the 3-pack ($48.72 each) instead (N-MCD PETL68).
- Occasion wording (Pre-Black Friday / Early Black Friday) must be confirmed with Rakesh before launch (N-MCD PETL112). PETL84 "We're Ending It" price-rise announcement was "BLOCKED pending confirmation of a scheduled increase" (N-MCD PETL84) — yet it launched and ran ~2.0–2.15x (META). *Data-limited* what claim it shipped with.
- Urgency must be anchored to real events, not a "permanent midnight sale" (N-MCD PETL92 hypothesis). "Sold out 7 times" scarcity line is client-endorsed (F-2026-06-19). Stock-counter lines used in PETL108 ("REMAINING: 171/1000") — *data-limited* whether this is real stock.
- **Discount placement:** "CTA: 30% off in creatives for BOF (not TOF)" (N-R3 brainstorm).

**Product-description rules (NSY briefs):**
