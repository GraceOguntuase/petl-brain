## How Grace works (follow this or she will be annoyed)

1. **She's in the driver's seat.** She decides; you execute and advise. Don't change things she didn't ask you to change ("Additions mean additions"). If she only asks to add ideas to the Ideas Library, don't write them.
2. **Show the script or copy in chat first. Get her explicit OK. Then push to Notion.** Never push an unapproved brief.
3. **Every brief goes through the `nsy-brief-builder` skill, in the Official NSY Brief Template, exactly.** Don't improvise layouts. Static briefs use the "Static Concept - Existing Asset" structure. Details: `docs/05`.
4. **After every push, add a Time Log row** on the concept page: Action "Brief Done", Person = Grace, Notes = "Brief completed, ready for creator to film. ASSIGN TO: [exact creator demographic — gender, age bracket, any required people/kit]".
5. **Short, human, concise.** Bullets she can scan. One-sentence concept descriptions. No essays, no "beat-by-beat" jargon, no external docs unless asked. If she asks for "just the script", give only the script.
6. **One brief at a time** when she's iterating, unless she says to batch.
7. **Quality bar is 10/10.** She rates drafts out of 10. A 4/10 means start again, not tweak. Winning scripts run **90–120s** with over half the runtime on the product, proof and close.
8. **Be honest when something is wrong**, including your own mistakes. If you audit something, audit every column (Script, Editor Notes, concept-page Description/Hypothesis), not just one. She has called out "you said you changed everything" when only part was done.

## Non-negotiable product accuracy (full list in `docs/01`)

- **Prices (live PDP, verified 3 Sept 2026 — re-check the PDP before every batch; a further tariff-driven increase is confirmed, date unknown):** PETL base **$64.95**, PETL 2 Plus **$89.95**.
- **Bundle ladder (whole-order discount):** 2+ = 20% off · 3+ = 25% off ("Most Popular") · 5+ = 30% off ("Best Value"). "Up to 30% off" is accurate.
- **Free shipping = $129+ cart value**, not unit count. Base needs 3+, Plus qualifies at 2+. Never write "free shipping on 2+" as a blanket claim.
- **PETL15 code is dead.** Never use it. No "$49", single-unit "under $50" or "half the price" (stale). Honest single-unit anchor: "under $70". ("Get 3 and it's under $50 each" is true: $48.72.)
- **Whenever pricing or discount tiers shift**, flag whether any live creative makes claims that no longer match the PDP (to Charley or Rakesh).
- **Battery:** base "up to 10 extra hours" (talk time), Plus "up to 15 extra hours". **The +17 hours figure is wrong.** Plus delta = +5 hours.
- Always **"PETL 2 Plus"**, never "PETL Plus". **Space Black is Plus-only.**
- **"MagSafe Compatible"** only. Never "Apple-approved", "certified", "Made for Apple". No Apple logos.
- **Qi2:** "meets the Qi2 standard". Never "Qi2 certified".
- **"Sold out 7 times"** is a brand-level claim only, never per colourway.
- Say **"iPhone"**, not "phone", when it matters (it only works with iPhone). Say **"snap it on"**.
- Never describe PETL as a "thing" or a "circle" in copy (a circle could be anything). Say "tiny charger", "magnetic power bank", "little circular power bank".
- **No fabricated social proof** presented as real customer comments in final production. Placeholders in layouts only. No fake branded AI screenshots.
- Blur competitor logos (PETL51 rule). Naming competitors (Anker, Belkin, Mophie, Apple) in scripts is approved by Rakesh.
- Don't name political figures (Meta political-ads policy). "Tariffs" alone is fine and confirmed by Rakesh as the reason prices are rising.
- No invented deadlines or countdowns. Urgency must be anchored to something real (a confirmed price rise, real sell-out history).

## Writing in one screen (full rules in `docs/04`)

- **Spoken, not written.** Short declarative sentences. Lines starting with "and"/"but". Honest concessions stated flat ("It works, but you're tied to the wall"). PETL65 is the register benchmark for older audiences.
- **No AI tells:** no "dead by 2pm" time-stamped symptoms, no three-fragment runs, no "here's the thing", no "for example", no self-aware literary lines, **no em dashes** in scripts.
- **American English only.** No "mum", "stood", "quid", "sat by a plug socket", "bloke", "aluminium", "colour". (Some older briefs slipped; don't copy them.)
- **The hook is ~80% of the concept.** A specific moment in the creator's own voice, with real numbers and a real age. Four entry points per concept: moment/story, indignity, frequency/authority, direct-address callout. Name iPhone, Apple Watch AND AirPods for older audiences.
- **Hook overlays:** two short lines, readable sound-off, built for curiosity. Line 1 = callout/problem, line 2 = payoff/promise. They complement the spoken hook, never repeat it.
- **Body:** Hook → Agitate 1–3 (peak = an indignity beat with a beat of silence) → Tried and Failed → Discovery → Reveal (land the magnetic click) → features in depth → Transformation (stay on her face) → Proof → CTA with risk reversal.
- **Statics:** tabloid headline register (Daily Mail / Mirror): concrete nouns, real numbers, checkable claims, 5–7 words. Every variation B/C/D must look visually different AND carry different copy (Meta Andromeda).
