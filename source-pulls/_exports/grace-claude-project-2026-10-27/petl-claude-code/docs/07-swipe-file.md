# 07 — Swipe File (benchmark scripts, word for word)

These are the account's proven scripts plus Grace-approved finals. **Prices and offers inside them are often stale.** Each one is marked [STALE: ...] where it conflicts with `01`. Swap in current claims when you iterate; keep everything else.

Two light edits from the originals: em dashes became commas or full stops (Grace's no-dash rule), and in the PETL70/75 hooks, "mum" became "mom" and "stood" became "standing". Everything else is as written, which means **these originals still break some current rules.** When you reuse them, fix:
- "this little thing" / "one little circle" (never describe PETL as a thing or a circle)
- British spelling and phrasing: aluminium, colours, "had got", "got me", ring, sofa, plug socket
- Three-fragment runs ("No cables. No hunting for outlets. No asking strangers...") — the PTL10 body predates the AI-tell rules
- "charges up to 10 hours" (it's 10 extra hours of talk time)
