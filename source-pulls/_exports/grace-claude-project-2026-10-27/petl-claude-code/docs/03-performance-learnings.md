**Losers**

## 6. Durable patterns (what the data keeps saying)

1. **Ranking and authority formats win.** Top 3 for Grandparents, Top 5 Ranked, the ChatGPT ranking. Concede a competitor's strength, then win on 3 devices + form + carry.
2. **The product must carry the back half.** Winners spend over half their runtime on features, proof and close. Thin "5 lines about the product" scripts don't convert.
3. **Multi-device charging is the #1 spec.** Show all three devices early (Rakesh wants them in the first 3 seconds of an unboxing).
4. **Price/bundle framing converts BOFU**, especially tied to an occasion. Plain "discount + small benefit" statics are worn out. Theme them fully to an occasion ("Pre-Black Friday Sale", "Pre-Halloween Sale").
5. **Feature-led beats colour-led** for statics.
6. **Apple as a foil works; Apple as an enemy doesn't.**
7. **Vague, clever or lifestyle creative loses.** Be literal and specific.
8. **One variable at a time** when iterating a winner.
9. **Situational = scroll-stop, not always conversion.** Only corporate gifting held up last round.
10. **Who's on screen decides delivery.** Pick the creator for the audience you want.

## 7. Dojo playbook checklist (Copy Dojo, 25 Oct 2026; run it each batch)
- [ ] Top 10 ads from the last 3 months reviewed for the shared pattern (only the top 10 matter)
- [ ] At least one close rip from PETL's own niche (power banks, MagSafe accessories)
- [ ] At least one rip from an adjacent niche (phone cases, chargers, tech gifts, desk setup) or a same-ICP brand
- [ ] One format PETL hasn't tried yet (formats are vehicles: skits, street interviews, follow-my-journey, whitelisted influencer, native pages, cartoon/Pixar/skeleton)
- [ ] One authority-led concept (tech reviewer, "phone repair guy rates power banks")
- [ ] One real-customer concept (call a real reviewer with a specific moment, script from their words, film seated to camera) or follow-my-journey ("A week of never hitting 0%", daily battery check-in)
- [ ] Max one AI format
- [ ] Landing page and tracking checked for TOF ads before blaming creative
- Make the mechanism visceral: the panic at 5%; a clean magnetic snap vs a bag of tangled cables.
- When the client pushes a format that isn't working: pull 3 months, split spend/ROAS by format and by who's on screen, and bring numbers, not opinions. If they insist, negotiate it down to the smallest share of the batch.
