Flag it to Charley or Rakesh; don't treat it as an editing task.

### Bundle ladder (whole-order discount, same % on both products)
| Qty | Discount | Base per unit | Plus per unit | Shipping |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | — | $64.95 | $89.95 | $9.99 FedEx 2Day |

- **Free shipping = $129+ cart value** (FedEx 2Day). Base needs 3+. Plus qualifies at 2+.
- The discount applies to the **whole order**. "Buy 3, Get 25% Off All Three" is right. "25% off your third one" is wrong.
- **"Your third one costs $32"** is valid only as the step from 2 to 3 on base: 2 units $103.92 + $9.99 shipping = $113.91, vs 3 units $146.16 with free shipping, so the difference is $32.25.
- "Get 3 and it works out under $50 each" is true ($48.72).
- The honest single-unit anchor is "under $70".

### Build & features
- Machined from a single block of aluminium, tempered glass top, zero plastic.
- **World's first circular MagSafe-compatible power bank.** Client quote at onboarding: "We are the first circular magsafe. 99.99% are rectangular we are the only circular."
- Self-aligning magnets. Strong hold ("shake it, flip it, it won't budge"). It sits flush. **Don't say rectangles "hang off the side"**; Grace couldn't picture it.
- Flat edge stands the iPhone up for StandBy mode on the nightstand.
- Charge shows in the iOS battery widget with no app needed. Fits iPhone 12+ (put on end cards to cut returns).
- Adaptive output and two thermal sensors prevent battery stress. This supports "tops her up so she's never running it flat". It does NOT support "PETL improves your battery health" (refused as a substantiation risk).
- Premium Apple-style packaging: tear strip, printed manual, USB-C cable in the box. The box side-art shows PETL on Apple Watch and AirPods. Packaging is the gift-ready argument.
- PETL fit on Apple's foldable **iPhone Duo** has been tested and confirmed (Round 8).
- **Charging three devices:** in Month 1 and Round 3, Grace said it charges all three "one by one, not at the same time". Later approved scripts (PETL65, the top ad) say "all at the same time". **Check the PDP before writing "at once / at the same time".** "Charges your iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods" is always safe.
- Apple Watch and AirPods charge **wirelessly** by sitting on top. Not via USB-C (an early Claude error that Grace corrected).

### goPETL app
- App Store: apps.apple.com/us/app/gopetl/id6752920475
- Shows live wattage, charging speed, time-to-full countdown and charging history. Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. No login, no account, no setup.
- It reads local data from the PETL on *that* phone. Never imply you can monitor someone else's battery remotely.
- **Grace's rule:** mention it briefly as an extra USP ("There's a free app too, where you can check the battery and see how long it's got left"). Don't weave it into the story. It isn't strong enough to carry a concept alone. Framing: "works with nothing, more if you want it."

- Ranking order convention: PETL #1, then Anker, Belkin, Mophie, Apple. iNiu and Ridge were dropped.
- **Always concede Anker's capacity.** It's the credibility beat that makes ranking ads work ("It holds a lot of charge, I'll give it that"). PETL wins on 3 devices, form factor and carry, not on mAh. Don't put PETL's mAh next to bigger banks.
- **Apple:** use Apple as a foil or as praise ("feels like something Apple would make", "the one Apple should have made"). Head-on "US VS APPLE" got 0 purchases on $185. Anker has no loyalty tax, so it can be compared hard.
- Category context: the market has moved to Qi2 25W and 10,000–20,000mAh. Competitor complaints are overheating, slow charging and bulk. Frame 3,000mAh as the daily top-up you actually carry, not a brick replacement.

## Claims you can't make (and why)

| Claim | Why not |
|---|---|
| Qi2 certified | Not formally certified; it "meets the standard" |
| +17 hours | Not on the live site; Plus = up to 15 |
| "charges up to 10 hours" | It's 10 extra hours of talk time. Say "gives me about another ten hours" |
| Free shipping on 2+ (blanket) | $129 cart threshold |
| PETL15 / $49 / single-unit "under $50" / half the price | Dead or stale ("under $50 each when you get 3" is fine) |
| Black sold out 7 times; "restock sold out in 7 minutes" | Brand-level only, or invented |
| "Airlines are going paperless" | Only Ryanair fully; US airlines still print. "Your boarding pass is on your phone" is true and stronger |
| "Apple is raising prices 30%" | 30% was memory's share of Pro BOM, not a price rise. Apple did raise Mac prices; iPhone rises are projected |
| New iPhone releases make old batteries die faster | Not true. Use "feels like" framing plus the mandatory "it's not a conspiracy, batteries age" line (PETL62). iOS throttling is tied to battery health, not releases |
| Smaller than a golf ball / smaller than an Apple Watch face | Untrue. It's "almost the size of a golf ball", "barely bigger than a powder compact", "smaller than your palm" |
| "Never die at the gate" | Self-harm adjacency; use "Your phone will never die at the gate again" |
| Free express shipping | It's FedEx 2Day free over $129 |
| Antimicrobial / hygiene (medical gifting) | Use "wipes clean" only |
| Charging-time claims ("back to full in under an hour") | Unverified |
| Custom logos / corporate pricing | Not a confirmed offering |
| PETL improves battery health | Substantiation risk |
| Unverified early specs: "charges to 55%", "5,000+ reviews" | Invented by Claude in Month 1. N52 magnets and "patent-pending" appeared in July website copy; check the PDP before using them |
